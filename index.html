Common Traffic Signs

STOP

Always stop completely before moving.

SPEED LIMIT

Drive within the allowed speed limits.

NO ENTRY

Vehicles are not allowed beyond this point.

ZEBRA CROSSING

Allow pedestrians to cross safely.

Safety Measures

Wear Helmets

Helmets reduce serious head injuries during accidents.

Wear Seat Belts

Seat belts save lives during collisions.

Drive Carefully

Avoid mobile phones and maintain safe distance.
